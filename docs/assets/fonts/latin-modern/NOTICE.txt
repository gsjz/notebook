Latin Modern Roman 10, version 2.004 (OpenType files, unmodified).

These three faces provide regular, bold and italic Latin text for printed
notes. Chinese text uses the separate Noto Serif print face.

Source: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern/
