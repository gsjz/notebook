# Postgraduate Interview Opening and Self-Introduction

!!! abstract "Overview"
    This note focuses on the opening part of a postgraduate entrance interview: greeting the professors, starting naturally, introducing yourself, and closing your self-introduction. The goal is to sound respectful, clear, and not over-rehearsed.

!!! note "学习提示"
    考研复试英语口语最重要的不是炫技，而是稳定、清楚、礼貌。开场和自我介绍可以提前准备，但不要像背作文一样一口气读完；要留出自然停顿。

## Greeting the Interview Panel

If you face several interviewers, these openings are safe:

- Good morning, professors.
- Good afternoon, professors.
- Good morning, everyone.
- Good afternoon, everyone.

If you want to add one polite sentence:

> Good morning, professors. Thank you for giving me this opportunity to attend the interview.

> Good afternoon, everyone. I'm very glad to be here today.

中文里常说“尊敬的各位老师”，但英语口语里不需要逐字翻译。`Good morning, professors` 已经足够正式；如果不确定对方是否都是教授，用 `everyone` 更稳妥。

!!! warning "Avoid stiff translations"
    Avoid `Respected teachers` in spoken interviews. It is understandable, but it sounds translated and unnatural. Use `Good morning, professors` or `Good morning, everyone` instead.

## When They Ask You to Introduce Yourself

Common prompts:

- Please introduce yourself.
- Could you tell us something about yourself?
- Please give us a brief self-introduction.
- Tell us about your background.

Natural responses:

- Sure. Thank you, professor.
- Sure. I'd be happy to.
- Of course. Let me give a brief introduction.

!!! tip "Do not repeat the prompt"
    You do not need to say "Yes, I will introduce myself now" in a mechanical way. A simple `Sure. Let me give a brief introduction` is enough.

## A Clear Structure

A self-introduction for a postgraduate interview can follow this order:

```text
basic background / academic interest / project or study experience / future goal
```

Useful sentence frames:

- My name is...
- I graduated from...
- My major is...
- During my undergraduate study, I became interested in...
- I have worked on...
- This experience helped me understand...
- In the future, I hope to...

中文自我介绍容易从姓名、家乡、学校、性格一路铺开；考研复试更建议把重点放在专业兴趣、学习经历和未来方向。家乡、爱好可以有，但不要占太多篇幅。

## Sample Self-Introduction

> Good morning, professors. Thank you for giving me this opportunity to attend the interview. My name is ..., and I majored in computer science during my undergraduate study. I have a strong interest in backend development and computer systems, especially operating systems, databases, and computer networks. In my previous projects, I mainly worked on backend APIs and data management, which helped me connect theoretical knowledge with practical engineering problems. If I have the opportunity to continue my study here, I hope to build a deeper understanding of computer science fundamentals and improve my research ability. That's all for my self-introduction. Thank you.

中文理解：

- `I have a strong interest in...` 用来表达专业兴趣。
- `which helped me...` 用来把经历和收获连接起来。
- `If I have the opportunity to continue my study here...` 比直接说 `I want to study here` 更委婉。

## Shorter Version

If the interview asks for a brief introduction:

> Good morning, professors. My name is ..., and I majored in computer science. I am interested in backend development and systems fundamentals. During my undergraduate study, I worked on several programming projects, which helped me improve my problem-solving skills. I hope to continue learning and researching in this field during my postgraduate study. Thank you.

!!! success "Core idea"
    A good self-introduction does not need to be long. It should answer three questions: who you are, what you have learned, and why you want to continue studying.

## If You Feel Nervous

Useful expressions:

- Sorry, I'm a little nervous.
- Let me start again.
- Let me phrase that more clearly.
- I may need a second to organize my thoughts.

Example:

> Sorry, I'm a little nervous. Let me phrase that more clearly.

这句话比突然停住更自然，也能给自己一点缓冲时间。一般不需要反复道歉，一次即可。

## Ending the Self-Introduction

Useful endings:

- That's all for my self-introduction. Thank you.
- That's a brief introduction about myself. Thank you.
- Thank you for listening.

If the whole interview is ending:

- Thank you for your time.
- Thank you, professors. I appreciate this opportunity.
- Thank you for your questions.

!!! note "自我介绍结束 vs 面试结束"
    自我介绍结束时说 `That's all for my self-introduction. Thank you.` 很自然。整个面试结束时，更适合说 `Thank you for your time` 或 `I appreciate this opportunity`。
