# CET-6 Part I Writing

CET-6 Part I 是写作部分，需要在 30 分钟内完成一篇英语短文。写作在试卷结构中占 15%；若按总分 710 粗略折算约为 106.5 分，但成绩报告中写作和翻译合并为一个单项，满分 212 分。

## 23.12

### 第 1 套：基础知识

!!! quote "23.12 (1)"
    Part I Writing (30 minutes)

    Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence **"As is known to all, gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is of vital importance for students to master an academic subject."** You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words (not including the sentence given).

#### 写作框架

!!! abstract "三段式思路"
    第一段承接题干首句，直接说明基础知识是深入学习的根基。可以强调任何学科都有核心概念、基本规则和常用方法，掌握这些内容后，学生才能理解更复杂的问题。

    第二段展开原因和例子。可以写基础知识能帮助学生建立清晰的知识体系，也能减少学习高级内容时的挫败感。例子可以选数学、英语、编程或自己的专业，例如学高等数学前要熟悉代数，写英语作文前要掌握词汇和语法。

    第三段总结做法。强调学生不应急于追求难题或技巧，而应先打牢基础，再逐步拓展到更高级的学习任务。

#### 关键句填空

1.  A solid grasp of the basics enables students to __________.
2.  Without fundamental concepts, advanced theories may seem __________.
3.  Take __________ as an example: before students can __________, they need to __________.
4.  Therefore, students should spend enough time __________ instead of __________.

??? warning "批改记录"
    | 不合理写法 | 合理写法 | 问题提示 |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | A solid grasp of the basics enables students to *face the difficulties efficiently*. | A solid grasp of the basics enables students to **tackle complex problems more effectively**. | `face the difficulties efficiently` 搭配不自然；写作里用 `tackle problems effectively` 更稳。 |
    | Without fundamental concepts, advanced theories may seem *unsufficient and inconvincible to be mastered*. | Without fundamental concepts, advanced theories may seem **too abstract and confusing to understand**. | `unsufficient` 应为 `insufficient`，但语义不适合；`inconvincible` 不能表达“难以掌握”。 |
    | Take mathematics as an example: before students can master *Fourier Transform*, they need to *learn the ropes of Calculus*. | Take mathematics as an example: before students can master **the Fourier transform**, they need to **have a good command of calculus**. | `learn the ropes of` 偏口语；`the Fourier transform` 和 `calculus` 的大小写更规范。 |
    | Therefore, students should spend enough time *on basics* instead of *rushing through advanced contents*. | Therefore, students should spend enough time **building a solid foundation** instead of **rushing into advanced content**. | `contents` 在这里不自然；`rushing into advanced content` 更贴合“急着学高级内容”。 |

#### 完整成文

**"As is known to all, gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is of vital importance for students to master an academic subject."** 【todo】
















### 第 2 套：老年人的社会贡献

!!! quote "23.12 (2)"
    Part I Writing (30 minutes)

    Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence **"With their valuable skills and experiences, elderly people can continue to make significant contributions to society."** You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal observations to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words (not including the sentence given).

#### 写作框架

!!! abstract "三段式思路"
    第一段承接题干首句，指出老年人并不只是被照顾的群体，他们长期积累的经验、判断力和责任感仍然有社会价值。

    第二段展开贡献方式。可以写老年人能指导年轻人、参与社区志愿服务、传承专业技能或帮助家庭教育。例子可以选退休教师辅导学生、退休医生参与健康咨询、老人参与社区治理等。

    第三段提出社会支持。强调社会应提供合适的平台，例如志愿岗位、弹性工作、社区活动和数字技能培训，让老年人继续被需要、被尊重。

#### 关键句填空

1.  Their accumulated experience can help younger people __________.
2.  For example, a retired __________ can __________.
3.  If society provides more opportunities for seniors to __________, they will be able to __________.
4.  Respecting the elderly means recognizing __________ as well as caring for __________.

??? warning "批改记录"
    | 不合理写法 | 合理写法 | 问题提示 |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Their accumulated experience can help younger people *make less mistakes and go on the correct road*. | Their accumulated experience can help younger people **avoid common mistakes and make wiser choices**. | `mistakes` 是可数名词，应用 `fewer`；`go on the correct road` 是中式表达，写作中不够自然。 |
    | For example, a retired teacher can *teach young people knowledge in his free time*. | For example, a retired teacher can **offer academic guidance to students in need**. | `teach knowledge` 搭配生硬；`his` 容易造成不必要的性别限定。 |
    | If society provides more opportunities for seniors to *join in social works*, they will be able to *give out their heat*. | If society provides more opportunities for seniors to **participate in community service**, they will be able to **make better use of their abilities**. | `social works` 不适合表达“社会服务”；`give out their heat` 是直译，不符合英语习惯。 |
    | Respecting the elderly means recognizing *their useful* as well as caring for *their bodies*. | Respecting the elderly means recognizing **their social value** as well as caring for **their daily needs**. | `useful` 不能作名词；`caring for their bodies` 范围过窄，也显得不够得体。 |

#### 完整成文

**"With their valuable skills and experiences, elderly people can continue to make significant contributions to society."** 【todo】

??? example "参考成文"
    **"With their valuable skills and experiences, elderly people can continue to make significant contributions to society."** To make the most of this potential, we should regard seniors as active participants in public life rather than as people who only need help. Their accumulated experience can help younger people avoid common mistakes and make wiser choices. For example, a retired teacher can guide students who lack learning resources, while a retired doctor can offer basic health advice in a community center. These contributions may not always be dramatic, but they are practical and meaningful. At the same time, society needs to provide suitable platforms for elderly people to use their abilities. Communities can create volunteer programs, flexible positions and digital training courses so that seniors can take part in social activities more conveniently. Respecting the elderly means recognizing their value as well as caring for their daily needs. When their knowledge and energy are properly supported, they can continue to serve others and enrich society. This is also a healthier way to build connections between generations.

### 第 3 套：儿童自主探索

!!! quote "23.12 (3)"
    Part I Writing (30 minutes)

    Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence **"Nowadays parents are increasingly aware that allowing kids more freedom to explore and learn on their own helps foster their independence and boost their confidence."** You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words (not including the sentence given).

#### 写作框架

!!! abstract "三段式思路"
    第一段承接题干首句，说明适度自由能让孩子在真实选择中学习独立，而不是只依赖父母安排。

    第二段展开理由和例子。可以写自主探索能培养解决问题的能力、责任感和自信心。例子可以选孩子自己安排学习计划、尝试一项兴趣活动、独立解决生活中的小问题等。

    第三段强调边界。自由不等于放任，父母应提供安全规则和必要指导，同时给孩子留下试错和成长的空间。

#### 关键句填空

1.  When children are allowed to make small decisions, they gradually learn to __________.
2.  This process helps them develop __________ and __________.
3.  For instance, if a child is encouraged to __________, he or she may __________.
4.  Parents should offer guidance and protection, but they also need to leave room for __________.

??? warning "批改记录"
    | 不合理写法 | 合理写法 | 问题提示 |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | When children are allowed to make small decisions, they gradually learn to *deal with things by themselves*. | When children are allowed to make small decisions, they gradually learn to **take responsibility for their own choices**. | `deal with things` 过于笼统；`take responsibility for choices` 更准确地呼应“自主”和“独立”。 |
    | This process helps them develop *independent ability and confident*. | This process helps them develop **independence and confidence**. | `independent` 是形容词，不能直接修饰 `ability` 表达“独立性”；`confident` 应改为名词 `confidence`。 |
    | For instance, if a child is encouraged to *try a new hobby alone*, he or she may *become more braver*. | For instance, if a child is encouraged to **explore a new hobby**, he or she may **gain a sense of achievement from practice**. | `alone` 容易误解为完全缺少支持；`more braver` 是重复比较级。 |
    | Parents should offer guidance and protection, but they also need to leave room for *children making mistakes*. | Parents should offer guidance and protection, but they also need to leave room for **trial and error**. | `room for children making mistakes` 结构不自然；`trial and error` 更简洁，也更像写作表达。 |

#### 完整成文

**"Nowadays parents are increasingly aware that allowing kids more freedom to explore and learn on their own helps foster their independence and boost their confidence."** 【todo】

??? example "参考成文"
    **"Nowadays parents are increasingly aware that allowing kids more freedom to explore and learn on their own helps foster their independence and boost their confidence."** This kind of freedom should be supported by clear rules and patient guidance. It gives children chances to make choices, face small difficulties and learn from the results. When children are allowed to decide how to finish a school project or how to manage their pocket money, they gradually develop a sense of responsibility. They may make imperfect decisions at first, but these experiences teach them to think independently and solve problems with confidence. For instance, a child who plans a weekend activity may learn how to compare options, control time and communicate with others. Of course, parents still need to set clear boundaries, especially in matters of safety and values. Their role is to offer guidance when necessary and encouragement after failure. In this way, children can grow through exploration, and their independence will become a real ability, not just a slogan. Such growth is difficult to achieve through constant instruction alone.
