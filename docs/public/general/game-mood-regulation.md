# Games and Mood Regulation

「一场手感差的排位后有感，顺便练习一下自己的英文写作能力。」

I had a bad ranked match in CS2.
During the match, my aim felt worse than usual. 2 of my teammates blamed me, but I was not intentionally griefing anyone. In one round, one of them also made a mistake that got me killed, but I did not argue with them. After it ended, I realized that a video game can be a sandbox for practicing emotional control.

## 随机排位的有限后果

中文提示：随机匹配、短暂冲突、退出游戏后不再延伸。

英文首句：A random ranked match may feel important in the moment, but its consequences are usually limited.

本段表达：这一局游戏不会天然影响后续生活，真正延长痛苦的是自己反复回想它。

## Differences between game and life

Conflicts in real life are harder to leave behind because people and memories often return in other situations.

In contrast, in video games, you are just match rating 相当的 teammates randomly, and you won't meet each other anymore after minutes. So handling such situation can be percieved as a necessary condition of facing the problems in real life.

写作提示：

- `match rating 相当的 teammates randomly` 可以改成 `random teammates with a similar rating`。
- `after minutes` 更自然的是 `after the match`；`percieved` 拼写应为 `perceived`。
- `necessary condition` 语气太硬，可以换成 `a small practice for facing real-life problems`。

## 没有恶意送分时的责任边界

中文提示：手感差、失误、没有恶意、没有故意坑队友。

英文首句：As long as I am not intentionally griefing my teammates, a bad performance should not become a moral failure.

本段表达：发挥差可以复盘，但不应该被理解成自己人品有问题或故意伤害队伍。

## 单排失误背后的信息和配合

中文提示：没有报点、没人架枪、补枪不到位、信息不足。

英文首句：In solo queue, many mistakes happen because the player does not have enough information or support.

本段表达：团队游戏里的个人失误常常来自配合环境，不能把结果完全压到一个人身上。

## 恶意压力本身的不公平

中文提示：队友甩锅、恶意指责、不给帮助、只制造压力。

英文首句：It is unfair when teammates offer little useful information but use an aggressive tone to blame someone else.

本段表达：恶意压力不是有效复盘，它把合作问题变成了对某个人的攻击。

## 好队友与恶劣队友的长期代价

中文提示：单排随机性、好队友的习惯、坏队友的性格成本。

英文首句：Solo queue also shows how different people carry their habits into a shared space.

本段表达：好的队友往往会把合作习惯带进游戏和生活，恶劣队友也会为自己的性格付出长期代价。

## 用游戏练习情绪复原

中文提示：坏回合、静音、下一局、把情绪放下。

英文首句：A game can become a small place to practice recovering from frustration.

本段表达：游戏的价值不只在赢，而在于练习从坏情绪里回来，不让一局排位占用太久的生活。
