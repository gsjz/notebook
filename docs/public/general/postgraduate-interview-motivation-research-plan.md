# Postgraduate Interview Motivation and Research Plans

!!! abstract "Overview"
    This note collects spoken expressions for common postgraduate interview questions: why you want to pursue graduate study, why you chose this major or university, what research direction interests you, and what your future plan is.

!!! note "学习提示"
    这类问题不要只回答“我喜欢”“我想提升自己”。更好的回答是：兴趣从哪里来、过去做过什么、未来想往哪里发展。英语表达要自然，内容要具体。

## Why Graduate Study?

Common prompts:

- Why do you want to pursue a master's degree?
- Why do you want to continue your study?
- What motivates you to apply for graduate school?

Useful expressions:

- I want to build a deeper understanding of...
- I realized that I still need more systematic training in...
- Graduate study will give me the opportunity to...
- I hope to improve both my theoretical foundation and research ability.
- I want to learn how to solve more complex problems in a systematic way.

Example:

> I want to pursue a master's degree because I hope to build a deeper and more systematic understanding of computer science. During my undergraduate study, I learned the basics of programming, databases, operating systems, and networks, but I also realized that many real problems require stronger theoretical foundations and research ability. Graduate study would give me the opportunity to learn more deeply and work on more challenging problems.

中文说明：

- `deeper and more systematic understanding` 适合表达“更深入、更系统”。
- `theoretical foundations and research ability` 适合表达“理论基础和科研能力”。
- 不要只说 `I want to improve myself`，太泛。

## Why This Major?

Common prompts:

- Why did you choose computer science?
- Why are you interested in this field?
- What area of computer science are you most interested in?

Useful expressions:

- I am interested in how software systems are designed and optimized.
- I enjoy solving problems through programming and logical thinking.
- I became interested in this area when I worked on...
- This field combines theory with practical applications.
- I find it meaningful because...

Example:

> I am interested in computer science because it combines logical thinking with practical problem solving. In particular, I am interested in backend systems and databases, because they are closely related to reliability, performance, and real user needs. Through previous projects, I realized that even a small design decision can affect the whole system, which made me want to learn more deeply.

!!! tip "Make interest believable"
    A good answer connects interest with experience. Instead of only saying `I like computer science`, mention a course, project, problem, or observation that made the interest concrete.

## Why This University or Program?

Common prompts:

- Why did you choose our university?
- Why do you want to study in our program?
- What attracts you to our school?

Useful expressions:

- Your university has a strong academic atmosphere.
- The program matches my academic interests.
- I am impressed by the research in...
- I hope to learn from experienced professors in this field.
- The curriculum and research direction are closely related to my goals.

Example:

> I chose this university because its computer science program has a strong academic atmosphere and matches my interests. I am especially interested in areas such as systems, databases, and artificial intelligence. I hope to receive more systematic training here and learn from professors who have rich research experience in these fields.

中文说明：

这类回答要避免空泛地说“贵校很有名”。可以说学校的学术氛围、培养方向、课程体系或研究方向和自己的目标匹配。不要编造具体导师或论文信息，除非你确实了解。

## Research Interest

Common prompts:

- What research direction are you interested in?
- Do you have any research plan?
- What topic would you like to explore in the future?

Useful expressions:

- I am currently interested in...
- I would like to explore...
- I do not have a very fixed topic yet, but I am interested in...
- I hope to first build a solid foundation in...
- Then I would like to look for a specific research problem.

Example:

> I am currently interested in backend systems and databases. I do not have a very fixed research topic yet, but I would like to explore how systems can be made more reliable and efficient. If I have the opportunity to study here, I hope to first build a solid foundation and then find a more specific research direction under the guidance of my professors.

!!! warning "Do not overclaim"
    If you do not have real research experience, do not pretend to have a mature research plan. It is acceptable to say that your direction is still developing, as long as you show serious interest and willingness to learn.

## Future Plan

Common prompts:

- What is your plan for the next few years?
- What do you want to do during graduate study?
- What are your future goals?

Useful expressions:

- In the first stage, I want to...
- I hope to strengthen my foundation in...
- I would like to participate in research projects.
- I also want to improve my ability to read papers and write technical reports.
- In the long term, I hope to...

Example:

> If I become a postgraduate student, I plan to first strengthen my foundation in computer science, especially in systems, algorithms, and research methods. I also hope to participate in research projects and improve my ability to read papers, conduct experiments, and write technical reports. In the long term, I hope to become a more independent and capable engineer or researcher.

中文说明：

`first... then... in the long term...` 是回答规划题的稳定结构。它比直接堆目标更清楚，也更像真实计划。

## When Your Background Is Not Perfect

Useful expressions:

- My foundation in ... is not perfect yet, but I have been working on it.
- I know I still need to improve in...
- To address this, I have been...
- I believe this experience also trained my ability to learn independently.

Example:

> My foundation in algorithms is not perfect yet, but I have been working on it by reviewing data structures and practicing problems regularly. I know this is an important area, and I will continue to improve it during my future study.

!!! success "Core idea"
    For motivation questions, show three things: realistic self-awareness, clear academic interest, and willingness to keep learning.
