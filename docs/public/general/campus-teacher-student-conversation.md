# Campus Teacher-Student Conversation

## Starting a Conversation with a Teacher

Useful openings:

- Excuse me, Professor Wang. Do you have a minute?
- Professor Li, may I ask you a question?
- Sorry to bother you. I have a quick question about...
- Could I ask you about the assignment?
- Would it be okay if I asked for some advice?

Example:

> Excuse me, Professor Zhang. Do you have a minute? I have a question about today's lecture.


!!! warning "Do not overuse 'teacher'"
    In university contexts, `Professor + surname` is often more natural than `Teacher + surname`. If you do not know the title, `Excuse me` plus a polite question is safe.

## Asking About Course Content

Useful expressions:

- I'm not sure I fully understand...
- Could you explain this part again?
- Could you give another example?
- I understand the general idea, but I'm confused about...
- Does this mean that...?

Example:

> I understand the general idea of caching, but I'm confused about when we should invalidate cached data. Could you give an example?

中文说明：

英文提问时可以先说自己已经理解了哪一部分，再指出困惑点。这样比只说 `I don't understand` 更具体，也更容易得到有效解释。

## Asking for Feedback

Useful expressions:

- Could you give me some feedback on this?
- I would appreciate your advice on...
- Could you take a look at my draft when you have time?
- Do you think this direction is reasonable?
- Is there anything I should improve?

Example:

> Could you give me some feedback on my research proposal when you have time? I would especially like to know whether the topic is focused enough.

!!! tip "Be specific"
    When asking for feedback, say what kind of feedback you need: structure, logic, topic, language, feasibility, or next steps. This makes the request easier to answer.

## Discussing Study Plans

Useful expressions:

- I plan to focus on...
- I would like to strengthen my foundation in...
- Do you have any suggestions for how I should prepare?
- What should I read first if I want to learn more about...?
- Are there any courses or books you would recommend?

Example:

> I would like to strengthen my foundation in operating systems and computer networks. Do you have any suggestions for how I should prepare?

中文说明：

问建议时，最好先说明自己的目标，再请对方给建议。只问 `What should I do?` 太宽泛，对方不容易回答。

## Talking with Classmates

Useful expressions:

- Did you understand today's lecture?
- Do you want to review together?
- How did you solve this problem?
- I got a different answer. Can we compare our approaches?
- Are you going to the lab later?
- Do you want to grab lunch after class?

Example:

> I got a different answer for question three. Can we compare our approaches?

校园同学之间的英语不需要过度正式。`Can we...`, `Do you want to...`, `How did you...` 都很自然。

## Making Small Requests

Useful expressions:

- Could I borrow your notes for a moment?
- Could you send me the slides?
- Would you mind sharing the link?
- Could you save me a seat?
- Could we move the meeting to tomorrow?

Example:

> Could you send me the slides when you get a chance? I missed the first part of the class.

!!! note "礼貌的核心"
    英文请求常用 `Could you...`, `Would you mind...`, `when you get a chance` 来降低压迫感。中文里的“麻烦你”可以根据场景转成这些结构。

## Ending the Conversation

Useful expressions:

- Thank you for your time.
- Thanks for explaining that.
- That helps a lot.
- I'll read more about it.
- I'll revise it and send you an updated version.

Example:

> Thank you for explaining that. That helps a lot. I'll revise my draft and send you an updated version.

!!! success "Core idea"
    Campus English is not about sounding formal all the time. It is about choosing a respectful sentence, stating your purpose clearly, and ending with a concrete next step when needed.
