# Literary Analysis Expressions

## Intro

In Chinese, a familiar analysis sentence often looks like this:

> 作者运用了比喻的修辞手法，生动形象地写出了人物的孤独，表达了作者的同情。

In English, the same idea usually sounds better when it is made more specific:

> The writer uses a metaphor to present the character's loneliness as a physical burden, which makes the emotion feel concrete and invites the reader to sympathize with him.

## Cheatsheet

| 中文说法 | English term | Useful expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 比喻 | metaphor / simile | The image compares A to B, suggesting that... |
| 拟人 | personification | The writer gives human qualities to..., making it seem... |
| 排比 | parallelism | The repeated structure creates rhythm and emphasizes... |
| 反复 | repetition | The repetition of "..." reinforces the idea that... |
| 对比 | contrast | The contrast between A and B highlights... |
| 反问 | rhetorical question | The question does not expect an answer; instead, it challenges... |
| 夸张 | hyperbole | The exaggeration intensifies the speaker's feeling of... |
| 象征 | symbolism | The object functions as a symbol of... |
| 讽刺 | irony | The gap between what is said and what is meant exposes... |
| 感官描写 | sensory imagery | The visual / auditory / tactile imagery helps the reader experience... |


## Common Chinese Analysis Phrases

### 生动形象地写出了

- vividly portrays...
- makes ... feel concrete
- brings ... to life
- creates a clear image of...
- allows the reader to visualize...

Example:

> The description of the narrow street creates a clear image of the character's trapped life.

### 烘托了气氛

Useful verbs include `create`, `build`, `establish`, and `intensify`.

> The dark setting establishes a tense atmosphere and prepares the reader for the conflict that follows.

`atmosphere` 偏整体氛围，`mood` 更偏读者感受到的情绪。`protagonist`：主人公。

### 突出了主题

Useful verbs include `highlight`, `emphasize`, `reinforce`, and `underline`.

> The contrast between the lively crowd and the silent protagonist highlights the theme of isolation.

### 表达了作者的情感

> The gentle tone conveys the writer's affection for ordinary rural life.

> The harsh diction reveals the speaker's anger at social injustice.


## Useful Sentence Frames

### Naming a Technique

- The writer uses metaphor to present...
- The passage relies on contrast to show...
- The repeated phrase "..." emphasizes...
- The rhetorical question suggests that...
- The personification of ... makes ... appear...

### Explaining the Effect

- This makes the emotion feel more immediate.
- This creates a sense of tension / loneliness / nostalgia / irony.
- This invites the reader to see ... as...
- This reinforces the idea that...
- This shifts the tone from ... to...

### Connecting to Theme

- On a deeper level, the image suggests...
- The technique supports the broader theme of...
- Through this description, the writer criticizes...
- The passage therefore presents ... not simply as ..., but as...

## Examples

### Metaphor

Chinese idea:

> 把时间比作河流，写出了时间流逝不可阻挡。

English expression:

> The writer compares time to a river, suggesting that its movement is continuous and impossible to stop. The metaphor makes the passing of time feel natural but also irreversible.

### Contrast

Chinese idea:

> 用城市的喧闹反衬人物内心的孤独。

English expression:

> The noise of the city contrasts with the character's inner silence, making his loneliness more striking. The external liveliness only emphasizes how emotionally isolated he is.

`contrast with` 和 `emphasize` 是写对比、反衬时很实用的组合。注意不要只说 A contrasts with B，还要补一句说明对比突出了什么。

### Personification

Chinese idea:

> 把风写得像人在低语，营造凄凉氛围。

English expression:

> By personifying the wind as something that whispers, the writer creates a bleak and intimate atmosphere. The natural scene seems to echo the character's sadness.

`echo` 在文学分析中很有用，可以表示景物、动作或意象“呼应”人物情绪或主题。
